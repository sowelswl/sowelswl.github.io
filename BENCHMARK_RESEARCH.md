# Investment website benchmark research

Point-in-time review: 6 August 2026.

## Direction

The site should feel like a discretionary trader and quantitative researcher's personal profile, publishing system, and evidence hub—not a generic résumé, an anonymous institution, or a return-chasing sales page. The most useful combination is:

- Artur Sepp's research authority and compact professional identity;
- Lyn Alden's clear reader journey from free insight to an ongoing relationship;
- Aswath Damodaran's durable, open research library;
- 有知有行's calm Chinese-language narrative and investor-aligned communication;
- Meb Faber's habit of turning one research identity into repeatable media formats.

## Benchmarks and lessons

### [Artur Sepp](https://artursepp.com/)

The home page quickly establishes role, research domains, professional evidence, and a chronological stream of recent work. The separate [research archive](https://artursepp.com/research/) makes expertise inspectable rather than merely asserted.

**Use here:** lead with the quantitative-investing identity, foreground AlphaForge, keep methods and live implementation adjacent, and make future notes easy to scan by date and topic.

### [Lyn Alden](https://www.lynalden.com/)

The site pairs a strong investment-strategy promise with a repeatable newsletter. The [free newsletter](https://www.lynalden.com/investing-newsletter/) states its audience, scope, cadence, and model-portfolio evidence clearly, while the [archive](https://www.lynalden.com/newsletter-archives/) compounds into a long-lived acquisition channel.

**Use here:** make the live report the primary proof point; publish on a dependable cadence; give every note a single next action such as reading the full report, reviewing the research, or starting a conversation.

### [Aswath Damodaran](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/home.htm)

The visual design is secondary to an unusually deep, open library of classes, papers, datasets, and tools. His [mission statement](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/biomission.html) makes generosity and teaching part of the brand.

**Use here:** build durable intellectual assets, publish methodology alongside outcomes, and favor reusable research notes and tools over frequent market commentary with a short shelf life.

### [有知有行](https://youzhiyouxing.cn/about/)

The brand connects investment decisions with a calm human purpose, then supports that narrative with education, tools, transparent product explanations, and explicit investor alignment.

**Use here:** write Chinese copy at eye level, explain risk before promotion, avoid promising future performance, and connect abstract methods to the decisions they improve.

### [Meb Faber](https://mebfaber.com/podcast/)

The podcast turns a recognizable research identity into a recurring distribution format and connects outside experts back to one investing brand.

**Use here later:** reuse one quarterly research thesis as a written note, chart-led social post, and short audio or video discussion rather than inventing separate content for every channel.

## Implemented information journey

1. **Personal profile:** name, doctoral status, private-fund experience, current role, and current focus appear before institutional branding.
2. **Path:** the transition from quantitative systems to discretionary trading and LLM-assisted decision making is explained rather than hidden.
3. **Evidence:** privacy-safe live summary plus strategy-versus-CSI-500 chart.
4. **Method:** human judgment, quantitative research, portfolio construction, AI support, and risk observation have explicit roles.
5. **Authority:** selected academic and applied research, including AlphaForge.
6. **Trust:** biography, methodology language, benchmark context, primary-source investment principles, and disclaimer.
7. **Conversion:** complete live report, research reading, or direct collaboration.

## Operating cadence

- Monthly: one chart-led observation from real portfolio or market data.
- Quarterly: one durable research note with thesis, evidence, limits, and implications.
- Annually: one transparent letter covering process, results, mistakes, and research priorities.
- Every release: publish once on the site, then adapt it for Zhihu, LinkedIn, and any future email list.
