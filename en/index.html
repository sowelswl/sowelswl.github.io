01 — Capabilities

Built through a complete chain,
not a personal label.

The public record focuses on methods, systems, and evidence. Weili Song is the named author responsible for the personal research and account records shown here.

Private-fund quantitative research experience with a solo, zero-to-one build across data, factors, models, portfolio optimization, risk, execution, monitoring, and attribution.

The research combines cross-sectional stock selection with time-series and discretionary timing. Models, market regimes, price-volume structure, and risk appetite are cross-validated; timing is live in a personal proprietary account for exposure management.

AI 2.0 and vibe coding support RSI-style recursive self-improvement: LLMs and AI agents help decompose requirements, generate code, test and debug, diagnose attribution, and design the next experiment. Core components are independently validated; human validation, risk governance, and deployment approval remain mandatory.
