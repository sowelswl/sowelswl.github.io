The person

Suya is my long-term research brand. I publish and take responsibility for its public judgments, methods, and records under my real name.

I am Weili Song, a PhD candidate in Management Science and Engineering at Hunan University, with 3 years of private fund experience. I independently manage proprietary capital.

02 — Method

Judgment asks.
Systems test.

Neither purely discretionary nor purely systematic: the method joins research direction, quantitative validation, and final portfolio judgment.

  1. 01

    Find viable directions

    Discretionary research identifies market structures, styles, and explicit invalidation conditions worth testing.

  2. 02

    Build quantitative sub-strategies

    Ideas become timing signals and auxiliary strategies whose stability and failure modes can be examined.

  3. 03

    Combine and size risk

    I combine sub-strategies and map market states into exposure appropriate for the portfolio's risk tolerance.
